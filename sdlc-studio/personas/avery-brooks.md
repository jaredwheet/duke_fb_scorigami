# Avery Brooks

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Cast role** | Primary |
| **Interface** | Duke football publication |

## Who They Are

Avery follows Duke football during the season and wants context quickly after a game. A previous
newsletter confidently reported an incorrect score, so Avery now checks whether unusual statistics
are trustworthy and whether the publication explains why a result matters.

## End Goals

1. Understand the final score and its historical significance quickly.
2. Receive useful game context and upcoming-game information on the promised cadence.
3. Trust that scores, records, and historical claims are sourced rather than invented.

## Experience Goals

- Feel informed without having to verify every number independently.
- Feel confident that an unusual score or story is worth sharing.

## Behaviours & Context

- **Environment:** Phone-first reading with short attention windows and intermittent connectivity.
- **Frequency:** Checks posts around games and reads recurring editions during the season.
- **Proficiency:** Understands football context but does not inspect code, database rows, or provider APIs.

## Frustrations

- A stale or duplicated publication makes the account feel automated rather than useful.
- A confident but unsupported number forces Avery to search elsewhere for confirmation.

## Scenario

After a Duke game, Avery sees a final-score post and opens the next newsletter. The post explains that
the score pair is new, and the issue adds a concise verified historical note and next-game context.
Avery gets the answer without needing to reconstruct the game from several sources.
